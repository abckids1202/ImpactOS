# Live Deployment Blockers

ImpactOS must remain in `DEMO` or restricted `CLOSED_ALPHA` mode until Pilar confirms each item below. The application must not invent these answers.

- Designated owner for private and sensitive reports.
- Urgent-help/safeguarding route and who monitors it.
- Authority to validate a problem cluster.
- Authority to approve surveys and identifiable data collection.
- OSIS versus administration authority to publish official updates.
- Authentication provider and account deactivation process.
- Pilot grades, groups, and student/mentor cohort.
- Allowed launch categories.
- Consent and participant-information wording.
- Retention period for reports, responses, evidence files, and audit records.
- Rule for sharing impact pages outside the school.
- Initial UI language: Bahasa Indonesia, English, or bilingual.
- Incident-response owner and support contact.
- Backup/restore expectation and rollback process.
- Final file scanning and authorized-download configuration.

No live-school readiness claim should be made while any critical privacy, safeguarding, retention, or authority decision remains open.
